## 🧭 About

**SYRIX VISION COMPANY** is a technology company and international community that brings together developers, innovators and digital creators to learn, collaborate and build modern projects with real impact.

SYRIX VISION is more than a community of developers — it's a genuine tech ecosystem built on **curiosity**, **excellence**, **professionalism**, **respect** and **teamwork**.

> Our vision is to become an internationally recognized company by **2030**, driven by innovation, teamwork and a genuine passion for technology.
