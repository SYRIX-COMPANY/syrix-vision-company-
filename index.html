About Us

About SYRIX VISION

SYRIX VISION is a modern technology community founded by Dawens (Inconnu Boy Sensei), Co-Founder and CEO, alongside DsPrimis, his right hand and Co-Founder.

Our goal is to bring together developers, innovators and digital creators from around the world to learn, collaborate and build useful solutions together.

We believe technology moves forward through shared knowledge, collaboration and innovation. SYRIX VISION is far more than a community of developers — it's a genuine tech ecosystem built on curiosity, excellence, professionalism, respect and teamwork.
